Training Bag

Type
Training Good
ID
thedigimod:training_bag

Usage

A starter bundle containing one each of the 5 basic Training Goods - Learning Table, Punching Bag, Training Shield, Target and Health Upgrade. Given to newly joined players by default (configurable).

Obtaining

Handed out automatically the first time you log into a world with the mod installed. Every new player gets one Training Bag along with their starter Baby I Digitama and a random Digivice, meant to get you set up with the basics of the training loop before you have to craft anything.

Handing it out is gated by the players spawn with training bag enabled config option, which is on by default. If a server disables it, new players will not receive one and there is currently no other way to get a Training Bag in survival: it has no crafting recipe, no loot table, no mob drop, and no block source. In that case the only remaining source is the mod's creative inventory tab.
