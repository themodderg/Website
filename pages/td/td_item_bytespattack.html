Sp.Attack Byte

Type
Stat Byte
ID
thedigimod:bytespattack

Usage

Directly raises a Digimon's Sp.Attack stat when used.

Obtaining

5% chance to drop from any wild Digimon killed by another Digimon (Rookie stage or later; Baby I / Baby II Digimon do not drop bytes). Rolls once per drop pass, and same-stage boss kills triple every roll.

Used on a tamed Digimon to permanently raise its Special Attack stat by 5.
